The free public nodes this site runs on refuse the call that lists a token's largest accounts. The authority checks above were still read live from the chain — only concentration and bundling are missing.

What the tool sees. Mint and freeze authorities, total supply, and the twenty largest token accounts with their real owner. When several accounts in the top 20 share one owner, it gets flagged — that is the most common bundling pattern.

What it does not see yet. Who bought in the first block, or who funded which wallet. That needs transaction history, and it is what version 2 is for. A green result does not mean a token is safe — only that these three traps are not present.

Clearing only reads public Solana blockchain data. No wallet connection, no signature, no transaction — the tool cannot touch your money because it never gets near it.

This is not financial advice. Most tokens go to zero, including the ones that pass every check above.
